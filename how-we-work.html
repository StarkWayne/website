How we work

An embedded team, not a project.

Most development is a project — specced, built, shipped and the team moves on. We don’t work that way. We embed: a senior team that learns your business and keeps building, with dedicated development time built into every month. Less a supplier, more a part of your team.

Straight to the people who build.

No account managers, no layers, no ticket queue. You work directly with the senior people writing your software — the same faces every time, who already know you and your systems. When something matters, an AI subscription doesn’t pick up the phone. We do.

“The support is second to none — just pick up the phone and you are straight through to a familiar voice who gets any problem solved with great urgency.” Courtney — Head of Transformation & Reporting, EDF Heat Pumps

We learn your business.

Before we build, we get to know how you actually work — your operation, your products, your customers. Month after month that understanding compounds, until you can bring us the problem instead of the spec and trust us to find the answer. That’s the shift from doing what you ask to knowing what you need.

“They just understand what we need better than we do most of the time.” Courtney — Head of Transformation & Reporting, EDF Heat Pumps

Fast, because we’ve done it before.

Speed is cheap now — anyone can prompt. Judgement isn’t. AI builds fast, but only for people who know what to build and can tell at a glance whether what comes back is right. Over a decade in production buys that: senior people who don’t need managing and AI clearing the repetitive work, so more of the time goes on the parts that are yours alone. Fast — and right.

You’re not starting from scratch.

Over a decade we’ve built our own platform, MPX — the foundation we build your software on. The groundwork already exists: inventory, orders, logistics, finance, reporting, the connective tissue most businesses need. We shape it to how you work, so you get software fitted to your business in a fraction of the time and cost of building from nothing. The platform is the tool; the team is what you’re hiring.
