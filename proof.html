Case study

An EDF company runs on what we built.

CB Heating was founded in 1999 by Clayton Browne, installing renewable heating — solar thermal, then air-source heat pumps — long before either was mainstream.

For years the business ran on spreadsheets. As demand for heat pumps grew, the spreadsheets couldn’t keep up: stock, quotes, installers, invoicing — all straining at the seams.

We built them a single system to run on. Inventory that understands how their products fit together. Automated purchase orders across suppliers. Quoting and multi-stage invoicing that flexes as a job’s design changes. Tools to run their installer network — job offers, diaries, onboarding. Digital signatures. Automated dropshipping. The connective tissue of the whole operation.

That was 2022. Four years on, we’re still building — because their business keeps moving, and so does the software.

“MPX has fundamentally changed and elevated the way we run our organisation. It is now the backbone of all our operational functions, from quotation and system design through to inventory management, logistics, installations, quality control, aftercare and reporting.” Courtney — Head of Transformation & Reporting, EDF Heat Pumps

It’s a close way of working. You get the people who build the software, not a layer in front of them — and over four years that closeness deepens.

“The Stark Wayne team deliver such a personal experience that it makes you feel that you are their only customer.” Courtney — EDF Heat Pumps

In that time, CB Heating was acquired by EDF Energy Group — Britain’s biggest generator of zero-carbon electricity — and became a key part of its green initiative. The software we built now runs an EDF company.

“In more than four years of working together, I have never once heard the words ‘we can’t do that.’ Their approach is partnership-led, and they consistently deliver exactly what our organisation needs to thrive.” Courtney — Head of Transformation & Reporting, EDF Heat Pumps
