Careers

Build software real businesses run on.

We’re a small, senior team that builds and runs software real businesses depend on every day — and has done since 2015. If you want your work in production, in front of the people who rely on it, this is that kind of place.

Serious work, and not much of a crowd.

We stay deliberately small. That means no layers between you and the work: you build directly for the businesses that use it, learn how they really operate, and keep building as they grow. Not throwaway projects — software that runs the operation, that you own and improve for years. And AI has changed the job. It clears the repetitive graft, so more of your time goes on the parts only judgement can handle.

Who we hire

Judgement, not job titles.

Senior by mindset

You can take a problem end to end without being managed through it. You know what good looks like — and you can tell at a glance when what AI hands back isn’t it.

Close to the customer

You’d rather talk to the person who’ll use the thing than read a spec about it. Great software here comes off the back of great relationships.

Fast, and right

You use AI to move quickly, but speed without judgement is just noise. AI makes us fast. A decade in production makes us right. The order matters.

No layers. No ticket queues.

What our customers get is what you get: direct access to the people doing the work, the same faces every time, real ownership of what you build. We invest in good people and keep them — we’re building a company that stands the test of time, not one that churns through it. No open-plan table football, no ‘work hard, play hard’. Just good people building good software, and being treated like adults while they do it.

“The support is second to none — just pick up the phone and you are straight through to a familiar voice who gets any problem solved with great urgency.” Courtney — Head of Transformation & Reporting, EDF Heat Pumps

How to apply.

We hire for the person, not a vacancy — so we don’t run a careers portal with forty tick-boxes. Email a human. Tell us what you’d bring and, ideally, point us at something you’ve built. If it’s a fit, you’ll hear back from one of the people you’d actually be working with.
