Custom software

Built in a hurry?
We’ll make it last.

AI has made it easy to build software. Keeping it safe, connected and running is still a job for experienced people.

Take it over

An app you now depend on

Someone built a tool with AI and it works. Now the business relies on it, and nobody’s sure how it’s put together, what it connects to or what happens when it breaks. We’ll take it on: find the weak spots, make it secure and keep it running.

Sound familiar?

  • Only one person knows how it works
  • It breaks, and nobody’s sure why
  • It holds customer data or takes payments
  • It’s never been security-checked
Build it new

An idea that needs doing properly

A new product, a customer portal, a tool your team keeps asking for. We build it with the same care as the systems we’ve run since 2015, quickly, and with someone to look after it once it’s live.

Good fits

  • A customer portal or self-service tool
  • An internal tool your team keeps asking for
  • Connecting systems that don’t talk to each other
  • A new product idea, built properly from day one
  1. We understand it firstHow it works, what it touches and what it’s for, before we change anything.
  2. We make it safeSecurity, connections and the risks nobody’s checked, sorted first.
  3. We build it forwardNew features as you grow, kept running and supported by the people who know it.
