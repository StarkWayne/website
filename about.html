About

Since 2015, and still building.

Stark Wayne is a development team — senior, close-knit and founder-led since day one. We’ve spent more than a decade building software for real businesses, including our own platform. Long enough to know what lasts, and how to build it.

Experience you can’t shortcut.

We started in 2015 and haven’t stopped shipping since. In that time we’ve built a platform that’s in production every day, kept moving as AI changed how software gets built, and learned the thing you only learn by staying: where software actually breaks — month-end, the stocktake, the Friday rush — and what it takes to keep a real operation running. Anyone can pick up the newest tool this year. Few have over a decade of judgement behind how they use it.

The same people, year after year.

Stark Wayne is deliberately small and senior. The people who started it still build in it — no revolving door of juniors, no offshore hand-off. When you bring us in, you get people who have built this kind of thing many times before, and who will still be here next year.

Why “Stark Wayne”?

Stark. Wayne. Yes, those two. We named the company after two fictional billionaires who build ridiculous things. We build the real kind — and have since 2015. The name still fits: you come to us when something needs building, and it gets built.

The order matters.

We came up building software the hard way — deep in business logic, careful design, real operations — years before AI arrived. That sequence is the point. AI makes an experienced team formidable; without the years behind it, it just ships mistakes faster. You can’t skip the first part. We didn’t.
