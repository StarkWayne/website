We’re Stark Wayne: the people who brought you MPX

Stark Wayne · 17 August 2026

You might know us as MPX, the software running your inventory, your orders, your reporting. Stark Wayne is the team that built it, and has kept building it since 2015.

We don’t usually put our own name front and centre. We’d rather the software did the talking. But we’ve been quietly busy, and it felt worth a proper hello.

Same team

Nothing about MPX is changing. The people who built it are still the people you deal with. That’s the whole point of how we work. You talk to the people writing the software, not a layer in front of them.

What we’ve been up to

Two things, mainly.

First, we’ve kept building. Some of the businesses we started with years ago have grown into something the original spreadsheets could never have carried. One of them was bought by EDF and now trades as EDF Heat Pumps. The software scaled right alongside them, because we were still there to build it.

Second, AI. Everyone’s talking about it. Most are still working out what it’s actually for. We’ve spent our time putting it to work, building faster, clearing the repetitive graft and reinvesting what it saves on the parts of a business only the people who run it understand. AI makes us fast. A decade in production makes us right. The order matters.

Why you’re hearing from us

Because MPX was only ever one of the things we build. Stark Wayne is a custom software development team, senior, close and in it for the long haul. If there’s something your business needs built, we’d like to be the people you come to.
